# RESEARCH — Did You Know No. 01 · "Time, Compressed"

Every on-screen number and every narrated fact traces to a line here. Researched 2026-10-10.
Story Council was NOT run (paid seats); research done directly with web sources below.

## Viewer prior vs. claim (Playbook rule 8)
- **Prior:** "Technology saves time, so we should have more of it" / "time just feels faster as you age."
- **Claim that violates it:** we have saved more time than any generation, yet feel time-poor — because
  every century compressed more into an hour, and the brain measures time by new memories, not clocks.

## Prediction framing (2100)
The 2100 scene is labelled on screen **"PREDICTION — a scenario, not a fact."** Only F16 is a published
projection; AI taking over much routine work and the return of Keynes's 15-hour week are presented as
scenarios ("is expected to", "could"), never as facts.
