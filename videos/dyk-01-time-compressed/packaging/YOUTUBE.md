## Description
In 1500, no message could travel faster than a horse. Today, one crosses the Atlantic in less than a tenth of a second. We have saved more time than any generation in history — so why does every one of us feel like we are running out of it?

Wake up in seven centuries — 1500, 1652, 1760, 1883, 1914, today and 2100 — and see how people really lived with time: sleeping twice a night, taking Mondays off, hearing noon strike twice in one day… and where all the saved time actually went.

Then we follow the evidence to the year 2100: ten billion people, AI and the 15-hour work week Keynes predicted. Is the time compression about to end — or get worse?
