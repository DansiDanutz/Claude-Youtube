# RESEARCH — Did You Know That? No. 02 · "The Sun"

Every on-screen number and every narrated fact traces to a line here. Researched 2026-10-11 (web search +
primary text where reachable). Story Council was NOT run (paid seats). F-numbers follow STORY.md; F16+ are new.

## Viewer prior vs. claim
- **Prior:** "The Sun is just there. I see it when I look up."
- **Claim that violates it:** five centuries of changing behaviour toward it (celebrate, measure, hide, split,
  chase, harvest, touch) — and you have never seen it as it *is*: the light is ~8 min 20 s old, and the energy
  in it left the core ~170,000 years ago (NASA estimate).

- "Galileo discovered sunspots" — false; naked-eye records are ancient, Harriot's telescope drawing predates Galileo's. Used as the era-2 twist.
- "Chanel invented the tan" — presented only as a probable myth (F8).
- STORY.md placed Galileo in **Padua** in the 1610s: he moved to Florence in 1610; the sunspot letters were written in Florence in 1612 → script says Florence.
- "Bonfire = bone fire / dragons" etymology — popular, unsourced → not used.
- Gunning sisters "mobbed in Hyde Park" — popular, not verified to a primary source → not used.
- 2100 (v2): no number past 2030.
