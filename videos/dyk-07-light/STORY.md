## 0. Logline
You wake up six times, from a Flemish town at curfew to your own window tonight. Each time, the light in your hand
gets cheaper, brighter and easier, and each time people use more of it. Then you find out what really happened when we
switched the night off: **we didn't save the light. We spent it, all of it, until it reached the sky, and the dark became
the thing that's running out.**

## 1. The spine: how humans saw light, rung by rung
**a luxury you make → a service piped to you → a liquid you buy → a network you plug into → a crystal, light without fire → so cheap we never switch it off → (2100) darkness is the luxury**

ordinary bulb for about an hour, Nordhaus L3). It counts down on its word, like No. 05's THE YEARS strip.
