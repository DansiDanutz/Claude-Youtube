Yet most money today isn't paper, or metal, or anything you could ever hold. So what is it, actually? In Britain, out of every hundred pounds people have, how many exist as real notes and coins? And in 2100? Cash is shrinking, more than nine in ten central banks in a 2025 BIS survey were exploring a digital currency, Europe is preparing for a possible digital euro (as early as 2029, if lawmakers agree), and the Bank for International Settlements warns that stablecoins fail key tests of good money.
